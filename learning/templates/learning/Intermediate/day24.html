R

🔥POWER WORD: INITIATIVE (In-ISH-uh-tiv)

Meaning: The ability to assess and act independently

Usage: "She demonstrated remarkable initiative by identifying the design flaw before it was flagged by the external auditors."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "HIT THE GROUND RUNNING"

Marathi Meaning: त्वरित आणि पूर्ण क्षमतेने सुरुवात करणे

Meaning: To start something with energy and without needing preparation time

Example: "The company was looking for a candidate who could hit the ground running and contribute from day one."

🧠 Grammar Hack: The STAR Method — The Interview Grammar

📖 SITUATION (Set the scene): Use Simple Past / Past Continuous

"During my internship at [Company], the team was working on a critical system upgrade when..."

📖 TASK (Your responsibility): Use Simple Past / Modal (had to / was required to)

"I was responsible for / I was tasked with / My role was to..."

📖 ACTION (What YOU did — use 'I', not 'We'): Use Simple Past (active verbs)

"I conducted a root cause analysis. I coordinated with the supplier. I implemented an interim solution."

📖 RESULT (The outcome — quantify where possible): Use Simple Past + Present Perfect

"As a result, the project was delivered on time. This has since been adopted as standard practice."

📖 Key Power Verbs for STAR:

Initiated / Spearheaded / Orchestrated / Rectified / Streamlined / Facilitated / Mitigated / Implemented
