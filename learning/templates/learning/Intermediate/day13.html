💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "PUT IT IN WRITING"

Marathi Meaning: लिखित स्वरूपात नोंद करणे

Meaning: To record something officially in a document

Example: "The client verbally agreed to the revised terms, but we asked them to put it in writing before proceeding."

🔥POWER WORD: SUCCINCT (Suk-SINKT)

Meaning: Briefly and clearly expressed

Usage: "Your project report should be succinct — no more than two pages, with all key findings clearly stated."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Formal vs. Informal Register — The Professional Upgrade

Informal Formal Equivalent
Sorry for the trouble I sincerely apologise for any inconvenience caused
Here is the file Please find attached / enclosed the document
Let me know I would appreciate your response / Please advise
Get back to me soon I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience
About With regard to / With reference to / Regarding
I want to know I would be grateful if you could inform me / Please could you advise

📖 Email Structure:


1. Opening: Purpose of email

2. Body: Details / Request / Action

3. Attachment mention (if any)

4. Query/clarification offer

5. Closing: Looking forward + Salutation
