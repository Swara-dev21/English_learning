🎧 1. Listening Activity · Word Stress Shifts

🎯Focus
Word Stress Shifts — the same words carry 3 different meanings based only on which word is stressed.
📝Listening Text
"She did NOT approve the revised design. (Someone else may have, but she did not.) / SHE did not approve the revised design. (Emphasis on who — it was not her.) / She did not approve the REVISED design. (She may have approved the original version.)"
1 Version 1 - Emphasis on "NOT"
She did NOT approve the revised design.
💡 Meaning: Someone else may have, but she did not.
2 Version 2 - Emphasis on "SHE"
SHE did not approve the revised design.
💡 Meaning: Emphasis on who — it was not her.
3 Version 3 - Emphasis on "REVISED"
She did not approve the REVISED design.
💡 Meaning: She may have approved the original version.

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "SPEAK VOLUMES"

Marathi Meaning: शब्दांशिवाय खूप काही सांगणे

Meaning: To express a great deal without words

Example: "The engineer's calm, confident tone during the crisis spoke volumes about her leadership ability."

🔥POWER WORD: ARTICULATE (Ar-TIK-yoo-lit / Ar-TIK-yoo-layt)

Meaning: Able to express ideas fluently and coherently

Usage: "He gave an articulate explanation of the thermodynamic principles underlying the system design."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Sentence Stress — The Meaning Carrier

Content Words (Stressed) Function Words (Unstressed)
nouns, main verbs, adjectives, adverbs articles (a, the), prepositions (in, on), pronouns (he, it), auxiliaries (is, was, have)

📖 Example: 'The ENGINEER has SUBMITTED the REPORT.'

Contrasted Stress (Emphasis) Example
Emphasize WHO 'The PROJECT team submitted the report.' (Not the audit team)
Emphasize ACTION 'The project team SUBMITTED the report.' (They didn't just prepare it)
Question Type Intonation Example
Yes/No Questions RISING ↑ 'Did you check the valve?'
WH Questions FALLING ↓ 'WHERE is the manual?'
