🧠 Grammar Hack: Defining vs. Non-Defining Relative Clauses

📖 DEFINING (No commas — identifies which one):

"The report THAT was submitted on Monday was approved."

(Which specific report? The Monday one.)

📖 NON-DEFINING (With commas — adds extra info):

"The report, WHICH was submitted on Monday, was approved."

(We already know which report; this is just extra info.)

📖 Important Rules:

• Use WHICH (not that) for non-defining clauses

• Use WHOSE for possession: "The engineer whose calculations were wrong..."

• Use WHERE for places: "The lab where we conduct tests..."

• Use WHEN for times: "The day when the results arrived..."
