💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "THE MOMENT OF TRUTH"

Marathi Meaning: निर्णायक क्षण

Meaning: A critical time when something is tested or decided

Example: "After two weeks of intensive preparation, this presentation is our moment of truth."

🔥POWER WORD: HOLISTIC (Hoh-LIS-tik)

Meaning: Considering a whole system rather than just its parts

Usage: "A holistic approach to engineering education integrates technical skills with professional communication."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Week 2 Indianism Fixes — 3 More Common Errors

❌ Incorrect ✅ Correct
'I will revert back to you.' 'I will revert to you.'
('Revert' already means to go back.)
'Please do the needful.' 'Please take the necessary action.' or 'Please address this at your earliest convenience.'
('Do the needful' is only used in Indian English.)
'The work is in process.' 'The work is in progress.'
('In process' = being manufactured. 'In progress' = currently happening.)
