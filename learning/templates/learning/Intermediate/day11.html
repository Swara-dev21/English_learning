🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Technical Seminar Opener

A structured seminar uses discourse markers to guide the audience. Practise these out loud.

1
"Good morning. To begin with, I would like to provide a brief overview of today's topic."
2
"Firstly, we will examine the causes. Subsequently, we will analyse the effects."
3
"In contrast to conventional methods, our proposed approach significantly reduces material waste."
4
"Having said that, it is important to acknowledge the limitations of this study."
5
"In conclusion, the evidence strongly suggests that this intervention is both feasible and sustainable."
🎙️Your Technical Seminar Recording
👉 WHY THIS WORKS: Discourse markers are the scaffolding of advanced communication. Without them, your speech sounds like a list. With them, it sounds like a structured argument.

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "IN THE SAME VEIN"

Marathi Meaning: त्याच पद्धतीने, त्याच विचारसरणीत

Meaning: In the same style or manner

Example: "In the same vein as our previous recommendations, we advise conducting a feasibility study before the next phase."

🔥POWER WORD: COGENT (KOH-jent)

Meaning: Clear, logical, and convincing

Usage: "The engineer presented a cogent argument for redesigning the load-bearing structure."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Discourse Markers — The 6 Function Categories

📖 INTRODUCTION: To begin with / First of all / Initially / By way of introduction

📖 ADDITION: Furthermore / Moreover / In addition / Besides / What is more

📖 CONTRAST: However / Nevertheless / On the other hand / In contrast / That said

📖 CONCESSION: Although / Even though / Despite / In spite of / Having said that

📖 EXAMPLE: For instance / For example / To illustrate / Such as / Namely

📖 CONCLUSION: In conclusion / To summarise / All in all / On the whole / Ultimately

⚠️ Key Difference:

• 'However' starts a new sentence (with comma).

• 'Although' joins clauses.

✗ 'Although it rained, however, we continued.' (Never use both together.)

✓ 'Although it rained, we continued.' OR 'It rained. However, we continued.'
