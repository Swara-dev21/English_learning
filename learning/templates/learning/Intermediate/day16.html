💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "CUT TO THE CHASE"

Marathi Meaning: मुद्द्यावर थेट येणे

Meaning: To get to the main point without wasting time

Example: "I know we have limited time, so let me cut to the chase: the project is three weeks behind schedule."

🔥POWER WORD: CONCISE (Con-SYCE)

Meaning: Giving a lot of information clearly in few words

Usage: "A concise summary is far more valuable than a lengthy report that buries its key findings."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Paraphrase Techniques — The 4 Tools

Technique Original Sentence Paraphrased Sentence
Synonym Substitution
Replace key words with synonyms 		The project was completed on time. The assignment was finalised within the stipulated schedule.
Sentence Restructure
Change the sentence structure 		Excessive loading caused the failure. The failure was precipitated by overloading.
Voice Change
Switch Active to Passive or vice versa		 The team reviewed the data. The data was reviewed by the team.
Clause Restructure
Change from a clause to a noun phrase		 Because the design was flawed, the building collapsed. The design flaw resulted in the structural collapse.
