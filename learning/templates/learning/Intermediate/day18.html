🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Group Discussion Masterclass

In campus placements and professional meetings, Group Discussions test your ability to enter, participate, and conclude.

1
"If I may add to that point — the data from the 2023 study also suggests a significant correlation."
2
"I would respectfully disagree with that assessment. In my view, the primary constraint is not cost but capacity."
3
"That is a valid concern. However, I would argue that the risk can be mitigated through phased implementation."
4
"To bring this discussion to a conclusion, I believe we have identified three key action points."
5
"I take your point about the timeline, and I would like to add that stakeholder alignment is equally critical."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "PUT YOUR FOOT IN YOUR MOUTH"

Marathi Meaning: चुकीचे बोलून स्वतःला अडचणीत आणणे

Meaning: To say something embarrassing or inappropriate by mistake

Example: "I put my foot in my mouth when I mentioned the budget in front of the client before the official announcement."

🔥POWER WORD: ASSERTIVE (Uh-SUR-tiv)

Meaning: Having a confident, direct quality without being aggressive

Usage: "An assertive contributor in a GD states their position clearly while respecting the views of others."

🧠 Grammar Hack: GD Language — The 5 Moves

1. ENTERING: 'If I may add...', 'Building on that point...', 'I would like to contribute here...'


2. AGREEING: 'I concur with the previous speaker.', 'That is a valid point, and I would add...', 'I take your point entirely.'


3. DISAGREEING POLITELY: 'With respect, I hold a different view.', 'While I appreciate that perspective, I would argue...'


4. CONCEDING: 'You raise a fair point.', 'I acknowledge that limitation.', 'That is a strong argument; however...'


5. CONCLUDING/SUMMARISING: 'To bring this to a close...', 'In summary, the key points we have discussed are...'
