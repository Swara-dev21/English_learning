🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Mock Viva

A viva voce (oral examination) requires you to respond spontaneously and with precision. Practice these questions.

Q1
What is the difference between a defining and non-defining relative clause?
A defining clause restricts the noun — it tells us which one. A non-defining clause adds optional information and is set off by commas. For example: 'The engineers who passed the test received certificates' (defining) vs 'The engineers, who passed the test, received certificates' (non-defining).
Q2
Can you give an example of hedging language in a technical context?
Certainly. In a report, instead of saying 'the result is conclusive', we might say 'the result appears to suggest a significant correlation, pending further analysis.'
Q3
How would you structure a response to a competency-based interview question?
I would use the STAR method: first, I would establish the Situation and Task, then describe the specific Actions I took, and conclude with the quantifiable Result.
