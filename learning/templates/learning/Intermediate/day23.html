🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Full Presentation Arc

Practise each phase of a professional presentation.

1
OPENING: "Good morning. Let me begin with a question: how much does a single structural failure cost in terms of human lives and public funds?"
2
PREVIEW: "Today, I will cover three areas: the causes of the failure, the remediation strategy, and the lessons for future projects."
3
TRANSITION: "Having established the root cause, let us now turn our attention to the proposed remediation strategy."
4
VISUAL REFERENCE: "As you can see from the graph on the slide, the correlation between curing time and compressive strength is clearly evident."
5
CLOSE: "To conclude, the evidence strongly supports implementing enhanced quality controls at every stage. I welcome your questions."

👉WHY THIS WORKS: Professional presentations follow a structure that guides the audience. Every phase — opening, preview, transition, visual reference, close — serves a specific purpose.

📖 3. Reading Activity · The Presentation Guide

Effective presentations are distinguished not merely by the quality of the content but by the clarity of their structure. Research indicates that audiences retain only 10% of what they hear but 65% of what they both see and hear. Therefore, aligning spoken content with effective visual aids significantly enhances comprehension. Furthermore, the use of rhetorical questions engages the audience and creates a sense of intellectual participation. Beginning with a compelling hook — whether a statistic, an anecdote, or a provocative question — can increase audience attention by up to 40%.

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "STEAL THE SHOW"

Marathi Meaning: सर्वांत जास्त प्रभावित करणे

Meaning: To be the most impressive performer in a group

Example: "Her clear, confident presentation on the new composite material stole the show at the technical symposium."

🔥POWER WORD: COMPELLING (Kum-PEL-ing)

Meaning: Evoking strong interest or attention; powerfully convincing

Usage: "The engineer gave a compelling case for why the bridge design required an immediate structural review."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Signposting Language — The Presentation GPS

OPENING: 'Today, I am going to cover...' / 'This presentation will focus on...' / 'By the end of this session, you will be able to...'


INTRODUCING POINTS: 'First and foremost...' / 'Let us begin by examining...' / 'Turning now to...'


TRANSITIONS: 'Having established X, let us now consider Y.' / 'This leads us to the second point.' / 'Moving on to...'


EMPHASIS: 'It is crucial to note that...' / 'I would like to draw your particular attention to...' / 'The key takeaway here is...'


VISUAL AIDS: 'As you can see...' / 'This diagram illustrates...' / 'The graph clearly shows that...'


CLOSING: 'In summary...' / 'To draw this to a close...' / 'I will leave you with one final thought:'
