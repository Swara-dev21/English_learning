🔥POWER WORD: CORROBORATE (Kuh-ROB-uh-rayt)

Meaning: To confirm or support with evidence

Usage: "Can you corroborate what the senior engineer reported about the load test results?"

💡IDIOM: "PUT WORDS IN SOMEONE'S MOUTH"

Marathi Meaning: कुणाच्या तोंडात नसलेले शब्द घालणे

Meaning: To claim someone said something they didn't actually say

Example: "Don't put words in my mouth — I said we should review the plan, not abandon it."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Reported Speech : The Tense Backshift Rule

When you report what someone said, the tense shifts one step into the past:


DIRECT REPORTED
"I am working." → She said she WAS working.
"I have finished." → He said he HAD finished.
"I will send it." → She said she WOULD send it.
"I can help." → He said he COULD help.

Reporting Verbs (upgrade from 'said'):

Stated, Claimed, Acknowledged, Asserted, Maintained, Emphasised, Advised, Suggested, Recommended.
