🧠 Grammar Hack: Reported Speech — The Tense Backshift Rule

When you report what someone said, the tense shifts one step into the past.

📖 Examples:

Direct: "I am working." → Reported: She said she WAS working.

Direct: "I have finished." → Reported: He said he HAD finished.

Direct: "I will send it." → Reported: She said she WOULD send it.

Direct: "I can help." → Reported: He said he COULD help.

Reporting Verbs (upgrade from 'said'): Stated, Claimed, Acknowledged, Asserted, Maintained, Emphasised, Advised, Suggested, Recommended.
