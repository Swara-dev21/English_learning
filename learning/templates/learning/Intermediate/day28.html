📖 3. Reading Activity · The 28-Day Reflection

Over the past four weeks, participants in the intermediate programme have been exposed to an extensive range of advanced grammar structures, professional vocabulary, and communication strategies. What distinguishes the most successful participants is not merely the acquisition of new knowledge, but the metacognitive awareness of their own language use. It is those who regularly monitor their own speaking and writing — questioning whether their language is precise, appropriately formal, and persuasive — who make the most durable gains. The skills developed in this programme are not isolated techniques; they form an integrated communication framework that will serve participants across all professional contexts.
