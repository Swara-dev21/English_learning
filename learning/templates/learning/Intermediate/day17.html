🧠 5. Grammar Hack: Double Negatives & Litotes — The Language of Implication

📖 LITOTES (Understatement using negation):

1. Instead of saying 'this is important', say: 'This is not without significance.'

2. Instead of saying 'the result was good', say: 'The result was not unsatisfactory.'

3. Instead of saying 'this was difficult', say: 'This was no small challenge.'

📖 Why use litotes?

It is the language of sophisticated academic writing and diplomatic professional speech. It allows you to make a strong point softly.

📖 Double Negative Rule:

1. In standard English, double negatives are INCORRECT in casual use: 'I didn't do nothing.' → 'I didn't do anything.'

2. But DELIBERATE litotes is grammatically acceptable and advanced: 'This is not an insignificant investment.' → 'The results are not without merit.'
