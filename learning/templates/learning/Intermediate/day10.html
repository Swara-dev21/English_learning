🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Research Presentation

Technical presentations require precise collocations. Incorrect word pairings make you sound non-native immediately.

1
"We have conducted extensive research into the structural properties of recycled steel alloys."
2
"The findings raise serious concerns about the long-term durability of the current design."
3
"Based on the data, we draw the conclusion that the composite material is superior."
4
"We strongly recommend implementing quality controls at each stage of the manufacturing process."
5
"This study paves the way for further investigation into sustainable construction methods."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "DRAW A BLANK"

Marathi Meaning: काहीच आठवत नाही अशी अवस्था

Meaning: To fail to remember or think of something

Example: "When asked about the technical specifications during the presentation, I drew a complete blank."

🔥POWER WORD: METICULOUS (Meh-TIK-yoo-lus)

Meaning: Showing great attention to detail and care

Usage: "A meticulous approach to documentation ensures that no critical data is overlooked."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Collocations — The 6 Key Engineering Verbs

📖 CONDUCT: conduct research / a test / an experiment / an audit / an investigation

📖 MAKE: make a recommendation / a decision / progress / an error / a contribution

📖 REACH: reach a conclusion / a consensus / an agreement / a decision / a target

📖 RAISE: raise concerns / awareness / objections / standards / questions

📖 SET: set a precedent / a target / a deadline / a record / standards

📖 DRAW: draw a conclusion / a diagram / a comparison / attention / inspiration

📖 Word Families:

• Analyse (v) → Analysis (n) → Analyst (person) → Analytical (adj) → Analytically (adv)

• Investigate (v) → Investigation (n) → Investigator (person) → Investigative (adj)
