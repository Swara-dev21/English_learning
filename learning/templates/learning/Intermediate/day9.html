🎧 1. Listening Activity · Gerund vs Infinitive

🎯Focus
Notice 'stopped using' (gerund — I stopped doing something) vs 'stopped to use' (infinitive — I stopped in order to use something).

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "FINE-TUNE"

Marathi Meaning: बारीक सुधारणा करणे

Meaning: To make small adjustments to achieve the best result

Example: "Before submitting the proposal, we need to fine-tune the cost estimates."

🔥POWER WORD: EMPIRICAL (Em-PIR-i-kul)

Meaning: Based on observation and experiment rather than theory

Usage: "The recommendation was supported by empirical data from three separate trials."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Gerunds vs. Infinitives — The Decision Framework

📖 VERBS FOLLOWED BY GERUND (-ing):

enjoy, avoid, suggest, recommend, consider, finish, mind, deny, postpone, keep, practise.

Example: I suggest REVIEWING the data before submission.

📖 VERBS FOLLOWED BY INFINITIVE (to + verb):

decide, agree, want, plan, promise, attempt, manage, afford, refuse, expect.

Example: I decided TO REVIEW the data before submission.

📖 VERBS FOLLOWED BY BOTH (meaning CHANGES):

• STOP + gerund = cease the action: 'I stopped smoking.' (I quit.)

• STOP + infinitive = pause to do: 'I stopped to smoke.' (I paused for a cigarette.)

• REMEMBER + gerund = recall a past action: 'I remember sending the email.'

• REMEMBER + infinitive = don't forget to do: 'Remember to send the email.'
