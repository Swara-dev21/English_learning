🧠 Grammar Hack: Gerunds vs. Infinitives — The Decision Framework

1. VERBS FOLLOWED BY GERUND (-ing):


enjoy, avoid, suggest, recommend, consider, finish, mind, deny, postpone, keep, practise.

Example: I suggest REVIEWING the data before submission.

2. VERBS FOLLOWED BY INFINITIVE (to + verb):


decide, agree, want, plan, promise, attempt, manage, afford, refuse, expect.

Example: I decided TO REVIEW the data before submission.

3. VERBS FOLLOWED BY BOTH (meaning CHANGES):


• STOP + gerund = cease the action: 'I stopped smoking.' (I quit.)

• STOP + infinitive = pause to do: 'I stopped to smoke.' (I paused for a cigarette.)

• REMEMBER + gerund = recall a past action: 'I remember sending the email.'

• REMEMBER + infinitive = don't forget to do: 'Remember to send the email.'
