📖 3. Reading Activity : Critical Analysis

Reading Passage:

The government's infrastructure policy, while ambitious in scope, has faced criticism from environmental groups. Proponents argue that the scale of investment is necessary to address decades of underinvestment. However, critics contend that the implementation strategy does not consider local environmental conditions. There is evidence to suggest that similar projects in other regions have encountered delays due to procurement issues. On balance, it can be argued that the policy's intent is sound, but its execution requires more rigorous oversight and stakeholder engagement. In practice, the success of such initiatives depends on adaptive management rather than rigid planning.

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "TAKE SOMETHING WITH A GRAIN OF SALT"

Marathi Meaning: एखाद्या माहितीवर पूर्ण विश्वास न ठेवणे

Meaning: To view something with scepticism

Example: "I would take the manufacturer's efficiency claims with a grain of salt until we have independent test data."

🔥POWER WORD: RIGOROUS (RIG-uh-rus)

Meaning: Extremely thorough, careful, or demanding

Usage: "A rigorous quality control process ensures that no defective component reaches the final assembly line."

🧠 5. Grammar Hack: Reporting Verbs for Critical Analysis

The Reporting Verb Spectrum : When analysing a text, the verb you choose shows your evaluation.


NEUTRAL (just reporting) : states, notes, describes, presents, identifies, outlines

AGREEING with source : confirms, demonstrates, establishes, proves, shows, substantiates

QUESTIONING source : claims, asserts, contends, maintains, suggests

DISAGREEING with source : overlooks, neglects, fails to account for, overstates, conflates

Examples:

1. The author demonstrates that the bridge design is sound. (You agree.)

2. The author claims that the bridge design is sound. (You're neutral or sceptical.)

3. The author overstates the structural integrity. (You disagree.)
