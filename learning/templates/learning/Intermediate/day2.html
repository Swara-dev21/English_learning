Meaning: Dependent on certain conditions

Usage: "The project's success is contingent on receiving the materials on time."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "CROSS THAT BRIDGE WHEN YOU COME TO IT"

Marathi Meaning: वेळ आल्यावर त्या समस्येचा सामना करणे

Meaning: To deal with a problem when it arises, not before

Example: "We don't know if the client will reject the design — let's cross that bridge when we come to it."

🧠 5. Grammar Hack : The 4 Conditionals — The Complete System

Conditionals are the logic engine of professional English communication.

🔹 Zero Conditional (Always true): If + present simple, ... present simple

"If you heat water to 100°C, it boils."

🔹 1st Conditional (Real future): If + present simple, ... will + verb

"If I study tonight, I will pass tomorrow."

🔹 2nd Conditional (Unreal present): If + past simple, ... would + verb

"If I were fluent, I would give a TED talk."

Note : Use 'were' for ALL subjects in formal writing

🔹 3rd Conditional (Unreal past): If + had + V3, ... would have + V3

"If I had started earlier, I would have finished by now."

🔹 Mixed Conditional (Past cause, present result): If + had + V3, ... would + verb

"If I had studied harder, I would be more confident now."
