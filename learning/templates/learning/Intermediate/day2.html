🧠 Grammar Hack: The 4 Conditionals — The Complete System

Conditionals are the logic engine of professional English communication.

🔹 Zero Conditional
If + present simple, ... present simple
"If you heat water to 100°C, it boils."
"If the motor overheats, the alarm activates."
🔹 1st Conditional (Real Future)
If + present simple, ... will + verb
"If I study tonight, I will pass tomorrow."
"If the test passes, we will deploy the system."
🔹 2nd Conditional (Unreal Present)
If + past simple, ... would + verb
"If I were fluent, I would give a TED talk."
"If I were the lead engineer, I would redesign this component."
⚠️ Note: Use 'were' for ALL subjects in formal writing
🔹 3rd Conditional (Unreal Past)
If + had + V3, ... would have + V3
"If I had started earlier, I would have finished by now."
"If we had calibrated the sensor, we would have avoided the error."

📖 Mixed Conditional (Past cause, present result)

"If I had studied harder (past), I would be more confident now (present)."
