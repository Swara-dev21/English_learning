🔥POWER WORD: IMPERATIVE (Im-PER-uh-tiv)

Meaning: Of vital importance; essential

Usage: "It is imperative that all safety measures be verified before the system goes live."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "BY THE BOOK"

Marathi Meaning: नियमांनुसार, काटेकोरपणे

Meaning: Strictly according to the rules and regulations

Example: "The inspection team insisted that every procedure be conducted by the book."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Passive Voice — The Complete System

Tense/Structure Example
1. Present Simple Passive The report IS WRITTEN daily.
2. Past Simple Passive The bridge WAS BUILT in 2010.
3. Present Perfect Passive The data HAS BEEN ANALYSED.
4. Past Perfect Passive The form HAD BEEN SUBMITTED before the deadline.
5. Future Passive The results WILL BE ANNOUNCED tomorrow.
6. Future Perfect Passive The audit WILL HAVE BEEN COMPLETED by Friday.
7. Modal Passive The cable MUST BE CHECKED / The form SHOULD BE filled.

Key rule: Only transitive verbs (verbs that take an object) can become passive.
