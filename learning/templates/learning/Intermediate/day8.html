💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "BY THE BOOK"

Marathi Meaning: नियमांनुसार, काटेकोरपणे

Meaning: Strictly according to the rules and regulations

Example: "The inspection team insisted that every procedure be conducted by the book."

🔥POWER WORD: IMPERATIVE (Im-PER-uh-tiv)

Meaning: Of vital importance; essential

Usage: "It is imperative that all safety measures be verified before the system goes live."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Passive Voice — The Complete System

Tense/Structure Example
📖 Present Simple Passive The report IS WRITTEN daily.
📖 Past Simple Passive The bridge WAS BUILT in 2010.
📖 Present Perfect Passive The data HAS BEEN ANALYSED.
📖 Past Perfect Passive The form HAD BEEN SUBMITTED before the deadline.
📖 Future Passive The results WILL BE ANNOUNCED tomorrow.
📖 Future Perfect Passive The audit WILL HAVE BEEN COMPLETED by Friday.
📖 Modal Passive The cable MUST BE CHECKED.

⚠️ Key rule: Only transitive verbs (verbs that take an object) can become passive.
