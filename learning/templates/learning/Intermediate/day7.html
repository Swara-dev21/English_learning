🧠 5. Grammar Hack: The Week 1 Indianism Fix — 3 Common Grammar Errors

Error 1: 'I am having a doubt.'

Fix:'I have a question.' or 'I need clarification on this point.'

(Stative verbs like 'have', 'know', 'understand' are not used in continuous tenses.)

Error 2: 'He gave a very good suggestion.'

Fix:'He made an excellent suggestion.' or 'He put forward a valuable recommendation.'

('Give' a suggestion is an Indianism. 'Make' or 'put forward' is the standard form.)

Error 3: 'We did a meeting yesterday.'

Fix:'We held a meeting.' or 'We convened a session.'

('Do' is not used with events. Use 'hold', 'conduct', 'convene', or 'attend'.)
