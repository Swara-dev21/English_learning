🔥POWER WORD: PARADIGM (PAIR-uh-dym)

Meaning: A typical example or pattern; a framework of ideas

Usage: "This discovery represents a paradigm shift in how engineers approach sustainable material selection."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "COME FULL CIRCLE"

Marathi Meaning: एक पूर्ण चक्र पूर्ण करणे

Meaning: To return to the original position after a series of developments

Example: "Our research has come full circle — we started by questioning the existing model and ended by confirming it with new data."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Week 3 Indianism Fixes — Advanced Errors

📖 Error 7: 'Please revert at the earliest.' ✗

Fix: 'Please respond at your earliest convenience.' ✓

📖 Error 8: 'I have given the presentation to the HOD.' ✗ (in formal writing, 'give a presentation' is casual)

Fix: 'I have delivered the presentation to the HOD.' ✓ (Use: deliver / make / conduct a presentation — not 'give'.)

📖 Error 9: 'He is having a lot of experience.' ✗

Fix: 'He has extensive experience.' ✓ (State verbs — experience, knowledge, belief — are NOT used in continuous form.)

Activity: Fix and rewrite these sentences:
