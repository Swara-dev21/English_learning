🔥POWER WORD: CONJECTURE (Con-JEK-chur)

Meaning: An opinion formed without firm evidence

Usage: "Without the test data, anything I say is mere conjecture."

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "ON THE FENCE"

Marathi Meaning: अनिर्णयाच्या अवस्थेत असणे

Meaning: Undecided; not committed to either side

Example: "The committee is still on the fence about which motor type to approve — it could be either option."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Modals of Deduction — The Certainty Scale

Express degrees of certainty with modal verbs.

1. 100% Certain (Present):

It MUST BE a wiring fault.
It CAN'T BE a software issue.

2. 90% Certain (Present):

It SHOULD BE fine after the reset.

3. 50% Possible (Present):

It MIGHT BE / COULD BE an overload.

4. For the PAST (modal + have + V3):

Must have been: "It must have been a power surge."

Can't have been: "It can't have been negligence."

Might/Could have been: "It could have been a manufacturing defect."
