🧠 5. Grammar Hack: Tense Synthesis — Using Past, Present & Future Together

Intermediate English requires you to combine multiple tenses in one paragraph.


Formula: Past (what happened) + Present Perfect (what you've achieved) + Future Perfect (what you'll achieve by a deadline)

Example:

I was nervous on Day 1 (Simple Past).
I have since mastered the basics (Present Perfect).
By Day 30, I will have developed professional fluency (Future Perfect).
