🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Negotiation Masterclass

Professional negotiation requires specific language patterns. Practice these out loud with authority and warmth.

1
"I fully appreciate the constraints you are working within, and I would like to propose an arrangement that addresses both our concerns."
2
"Rather than a complete schedule revision, might we consider a phased delivery, with the first milestone brought forward by two weeks?"
3
"I am prepared to accept that condition, provided that the revised specifications are confirmed in writing by Friday."
4
"I think we can find common ground here. Our fundamental interest is the same: a successful outcome for both parties."
5
"Should there be any ambiguity in the final terms, I would recommend we refer to the original contract language as the binding reference."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Negotiation Language — The 5 Moves

1. ACKNOWLEDGE THE OTHER POSITION: 'I fully understand your perspective on this.' 'I appreciate that this represents a significant concern for your team.'


2. STATE YOUR POSITION CALMLY: 'From our perspective, the primary issue is...' 'Our position is that the original specification is binding.'


3. PROPOSE A MIDDLE GROUND: 'Perhaps we could consider...', 'What if we were to...', 'Might it be possible to...' 'Would you be open to a phased approach?'


4. CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE: 'I would be prepared to accept that, provided that...' 'We could agree to that condition, on the understanding that...'


5. CONFIRM AGREEMENT: 'I believe we have reached an agreement in principle.' 'Let us put the key terms in writing and have both parties confirm.'

💡IDIOM OF THE DAY: "MEET SOMEONE HALFWAY"

Marathi Meaning: मध्यभागी येऊन तोडगा काढणे

Meaning: To compromise with someone

Example: "We can't accept the full schedule change, but we're willing to meet you halfway and bring the first milestone forward by two weeks."

🔥POWER WORD: CONSENSUS (Kun-SEN-sus)

Meaning: A general agreement reached by a group

Usage: "After three rounds of discussion, the committee reached a consensus on the revised project timeline."
