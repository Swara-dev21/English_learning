📝 ADVANCED WRITING — THE STRUCTURED ARGUMENT

💡 "An argument without structure is an opinion. Structure makes it a case."
💡

TIP OF THE DAY

Advanced argumentation always anticipates the counterargument. Before you state your conclusion, write down the strongest objection to your position — then answer it. This technique, called 'pre-empting the counterargument', makes your writing bulletproof and demonstrates intellectual maturity to your reader.

📖 3. Reading Activity · The Bridge Metaphor

Reading Passage:

A well-structured argument is like a well-built bridge: every component serves a purpose, and the removal of any single element causes the whole structure to weaken. The claim is the span — it crosses from the current situation to the desired conclusion. The evidence is the support pillars — without them, the claim collapses under scrutiny. The reasoning is the material that connects evidence to claim: it explains why the evidence matters. And the conclusion is the landing — it tells the reader exactly where they have arrived and what they should do next. Advanced writers also include a counterargument — not as a concession of weakness, but as a demonstration of intellectual confidence.

🏗️ Bridge = Argument Structure

Claim = Span → Evidence = Pillars → Conclusion = Landing
Hint: It shows strength, not weakness, by anticipating objections.
Hint: Start with "Not all students..." or "Some might argue..."

💡IDIOM: "STAND YOUR GROUND"

Meaning: Maintain your position under pressure

Example: "When questioned, the engineer stood her ground and presented the data."

🔥POWER WORD: INCONTESTABLE

Meaning: Not able to be denied or disputed

Usage: "The results provided incontestable proof of superiority."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Avoid Repetitive 'I'


❌ Weak: "I analysed the data. I found anomalies. I reported them."


✅ Strong: "After analysing the data, anomalies were found and reported."


Alternative openers: 'The analysis revealed...', 'Upon investigation...'


Rewrite to avoid starting with 'I':


"I reviewed the results. I noticed a pattern. I recommended a diagnostic."
