TIP OF THE DAY

Every technical problem you have solved is a story waiting to be told. Use the SCA framework: Situation (what was the context?), Complication (what went wrong?), Action (what did you do?). Practise framing one past project using SCA — it will become your most powerful interview and presentation tool.

🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The Engineering Story

🎯Task
Deliver a 60-90 second story about a real or hypothetical technical problem using the SCA structure.
📋 Story Structure Template:
• "The situation was... [set the scene]"
• "The complication arose when... [what went wrong]"
• "I analysed and realised that... [your insight]"
• "As a result... [measurable outcome]"
Example
"The situation was a motor driver overheating. The complication arose when temperature exceeded limits. I analysed and found a faulty resistor. As a result, temperature dropped 40%."

📖 3. Reading Activity · The Power of Narrative

Reading Passage:

Engineers are trained to think in systems, diagrams, and data. But the most impactful engineers are those who can translate that technical thinking into a story that a non-engineer can act on. A story does what a specification cannot: it creates empathy, suspense, and investment. When you tell a decision-maker about a system failure as a narrative — with characters, tension, and resolution — they remember it. When you give them a data table, they file it away. The engineer who masters storytelling gains an influence that pure technical skill cannot.

✍️ 4. Writing Activity · Technical Problem Story

📝Task
Write a 150-word story about solving a technical problem. Include: challenge, your action, result. Use at least two narrative tenses (past perfect + past simple) and one vocabulary word from today's list.
💡 Writing Tip

Use past perfect tense ('had been stalling', 'had not been tested') to show that something happened before the main story event. This creates professional depth in technical narratives.

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Avoid Sentence Fragments

📖 Why to avoid sentence fragments:

A sentence must have a subject and a verb. Fragments are incomplete thoughts that mislead readers.

❌ Incorrect (Fragment): "Because the voltage dropped suddenly."

✅ Correct: "The system shut down because the voltage dropped suddenly."


❌ Incorrect (Fragment): "The engineer. Working on the project alone."

✅ Correct: "The engineer was working on the project alone."


Rule: Before submitting any writing, check that every sentence can stand alone with a complete thought.

⚠️ Activity: Fix these sentence fragments:

2. "Because the data was inconclusive. We ran the test again."
