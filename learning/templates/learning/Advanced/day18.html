PASSIVE VOICE MASTERY

💬 "The passive voice does not hide action — it redirects focus to what matters most."
💡 TIP OF THE DAY

The passive voice is not a weakness — it is a focus tool. Use it when the action or result is more important than the actor: 'The bridge was constructed in 1998' (focus: bridge). Use active when the actor is more important: 'The chief engineer designed the solution' (focus: engineer). Mix both intentionally, never accidentally.

📖 3. Reading · Surgical Precision

Academic and technical writing in English has a long tradition of passive voice — not as laziness, but as deliberate objectivity. When a scientific paper writes 'the experiment was conducted', it signals that the method matters, not the individual who conducted it. However, over-use of the passive voice creates distance and confusion. Readers lose track of who is responsible for what. The advanced writer uses passive voice with surgical precision: for processes, results, and procedures where the action is paramount; and active voice for human agency, accountability, and leadership.

(a) Why does academic writing favour passive voice?

(b) When does the author say over-use of passive voice creates problems?

(c) Rewrite with balance: 'The task was assigned. The work was done. The report was submitted. The findings were presented.'

🔥POWER WORD: OBJECTIVE

Meaning: Not influenced by personal feelings; impartial

Usage: "An objective assessment of the data led to a better solution."

💬IDIOM: "Carry the weight"

Meaning: Bear significant responsibility

Example: "As project lead, she carried the weight of the entire system integration phase."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Control Your Emphasis

Passive for: results ('The data was collected'), processes ('The voltage was measured'), and formal reports. Active for: agency ('The engineer diagnosed the fault'), leadership ('The team implemented the solution'), and direct responsibility.

Test: If you can add 'by someone' to a passive sentence and the sentence becomes more informative, consider switching to active to name the actor.
