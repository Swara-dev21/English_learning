🧠 6. Grammar Hack : Relative Clauses — Adding Precision

A relative clause adds information about a noun using which, who, that, whose, where.
They make sentences more precise and professional. Review the examples:


The engineer fixed the bug.  →  The engineer, who had identified the root cause, fixed the bug.

The report was submitted late.  →  The report, which lacked proper data validation, was submitted late.

The design was rejected.  →  The design, whose specifications were incomplete, was rejected.
