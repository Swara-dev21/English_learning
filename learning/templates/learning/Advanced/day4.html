TIP OF THE DAY

Never open a presentation with your name and topic title — the audience already knows it. Open with a startling statistic, a thought-provoking question, or a one-sentence problem statement. The first 15 seconds determine whether you have the room.

📖 3. Reading Activity : The Art of Signposting

Passage :
The best technical presenters share one quality: they never assume the audience already knows. They build a bridge from the known to the unknown — starting with a familiar concept and expanding outward. A presentation without signposting is like a city without road signs: the destination exists, but no one can reach it. Mastery lies in guiding, not just informing. Your audience should never wonder where they are in your explanation.

🔥POWER WORD: COGENT (KOH-jent)

Meaning: Clear, logical, and convincing

Usage: "His cogent explanation of the failure mode impressed the entire panel."

💡IDIOM: "Paint the big picture"

Meaning: Explain the overall vision before details

Example: "Before we dive into the schematics, let me paint the big picture."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack : Present Continuous Overuse

Indian speakers often use present continuous where simple present is correct. Review the examples:


The motor is consuming 5 amps normally. → The motor consumes 5 amps normally.

He is knowing the answer. → He knows the answer.

I am understanding the concept. → I understand the concept.

🔷 Activity: Correct these sentences (use simple present instead of continuous):


1. "The sensor is detecting temperature changes normally."


2. "She is having a degree in mechanical engineering."


3. "We are needing to calibrate the equipment every morning."
