🔥POWER WORD: INCONTROVERTIBLE

Meaning: Impossible to deny or dispute

Usage: "The incontrovertible evidence supports our design choice."

💡IDIOM: "CUT TO THE CHASE"

Meaning: Get to the point directly

Example: "Let me cut to the chase: we need a 15% cost reduction by Q3."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack : Avoiding Redundant Intensifiers

Many Indian speakers add unnecessary intensifiers — 'very unique', 'completely finish', 'return back'.


Please revert back to me. → Please revert to me.

The project is totally completed. → The project is complete.

I am very much agreed. → I agree entirely.

🔷 Activity: Fix these redundant intensifier errors:


1. "The new design is very unique and innovative."


2. "Let us discuss about the project timeline."


3. "I need to return back the book by tomorrow."
