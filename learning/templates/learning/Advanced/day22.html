📊 WRITING COMPLEX REPORTS — STRUCTURE AND FLOW

💡 "A report is not a dump of information — it is a guided journey for the reader."

TIP OF THE DAY

Before writing any report, create an 'executive reading path': the first sentence of each paragraph should be strong enough that a busy decision-maker reading only those sentences gets the full picture. If they do, your report has good structure.

🎧 1. Listening Activity · Report Structure

🎯Focus
Report Structure — how professional reports guide the reader through information.
📝Listening Text
"This report analyses the cause of the August cooling system failure and recommends three corrective measures. The primary cause was identified as inadequate thermal management during peak load periods. Secondary contributing factors included deferred maintenance and insufficient monitoring frequency. Based on these findings, we recommend: (1) installation of an automated thermal monitoring system, (2) a revised maintenance schedule, and (3) operator training on early-warning indicators."

📖 3. Reading Activity · Report as Reader's Journey

Reading Passage:

A well-structured report is an act of respect for the reader's time. Every section should earn its place by answering a question the reader is likely to have at that moment in their reading journey. The executive summary answers 'What do I need to know right now?' The introduction answers 'Why are we examining this?' The findings section answers 'What did we discover?' The recommendations answer 'What should we do?' The conclusion answers 'What is the most important takeaway?' Advanced report writers also master transitions: the sentence that ends one section and introduces the next should be a bridge — summarising what has just been established and signalling what comes next.

✍️ 4. Writing Activity · Structured Report Section

📝Task
Write a 100-word structured section of a technical report: Introduction → Problem Statement → Primary Findings → Recommendations. Use formal, impersonal language. Include one transition sentence between sections.
💡 Writing Tip

The 'inverted pyramid' principle applies to report writing: lead with the most important information, then support it. Busy readers make decisions after reading the first paragraph.

Meaning: Bring separate pieces of information together into a clear picture

Example: "The report's strength was its ability to connect the dots between the three separate failure events."

Meaning: Done or acting according to a fixed plan or method

Usage: "A systematic report structure helps readers find key information efficiently."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: One Main Idea Per Paragraph

📖 The Rule: Every paragraph must have ONE main idea.


❌ Incorrect (Paragraph Overloading): "The cooling system failed due to thermal overload. Maintenance records show three missed inspections. The operator error contributed to the failure. The budget impact is estimated at $50,000."


✅ Correct (Split into 4 paragraphs):
Paragraph 1: Cause of failure — thermal overload.
Paragraph 2: Contributing factor — missed maintenance inspections.
Paragraph 3: Contributing factor — operator error.
Paragraph 4: Consequence — budget impact of $50,000.


Test: If you can write two different headings for the same paragraph, split it. Each heading = one paragraph.
