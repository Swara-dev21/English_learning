📖 3. Reading Activity : The Dance of Negotiation

Passage :
Negotiation is not a battle — it is a dance. The most effective negotiators are not those who demand the most, but those who listen the most. They understand that every 'no' contains the seed of a 'yes' — they simply need to find the right conditions. Advanced communicators replace 'you must' with 'it would be beneficial if', and transform ultimatums into invitations. This shift in language does not weaken your position. It reinforces it by making cooperation the more attractive option.

🧠 6. Grammar Hack : Overuse of 'Kindly'

'Kindly' is overused in Indian professional English and sounds outdated globally. Review the examples:


Kindly do the needful.  →  Please take the necessary action.

Kindly revert at the earliest.  →  Please respond at your earliest convenience.

Kindly note the same.  →  Please take note.

🔷 Activity: Replace 'kindly' with modern professional alternatives:


1. "Kindly send the documents by tomorrow."


2. "Kindly update the status at the earliest."


3. "Kindly do the needful regarding the pending invoice."
