🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "RESILIENT"

Pronunciation: Re-ZIL-yent

Meaning: Able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions.

Usage: "The team remained resilient even after the project was delayed twice."

💡Proverbs: Marathi vs. English

1. Marathi: "एका हाताने टाळी वाजत नाही."
English: "It takes two to tango." (Both parties are responsible for a situation/conflict).

2. Marathi: "गरज सरो आणि वैद्य मरो."
English: "Danger past, God forgotten." (People forget those who helped them once the problem is solved).

3. Marathi: "थेंबे थेंबे तळे साचे."
English: "Many a mickle makes a muckle" or "Small strokes fell great oaks." (Small consistent efforts lead to big results).

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "So" vs. "Therefore" Professionalism

🔹 The Hack: Upgrade informal "So" to professional conjunctive adverbs.

1. Instead of "So, we need more time":

"Consequently, we require an extension."

2. Instead of "But, the budget is low":

"However, the budget is limited."

3. Instead of "Also, I checked the site":

"Furthermore, I have perused the site."

✍️ Activity: Rewrite these "Common" sentences into "Executive" English

1. "So, I will call you tomorrow."

2. "But, the results are strange."

3. "Also, please send the file."
