2. Marathi: "गरज सरो आणि वैद्य मरो."

3. Marathi: "थेंबे थेंबे तळे साचे."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "So" vs. "Therefore" Professionalism

🔹 The Hack: Upgrade informal "So" to professional conjunctive adverbs.

1. Instead of "So, we need more time":

"Consequently, we require an extension."

2. Instead of "But, the budget is low":

"However, the budget is limited."

3. Instead of "Also, I checked the site":

"Furthermore, I have perused the site."
