🧠 Grammar Hack: The "Power Word" Chain

🔹 The Hack: To sound more sophisticated, connect your ideas using Signposting words. Avoid using "And" or "But" at the start of every sentence.

📝 Example:

Initially, we identified the bottleneck.

Furthermore, we scrutinized the data to find a feasible fix.

Consequently, the output augmented by 20%.
