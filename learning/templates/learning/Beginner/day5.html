🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Street Interviewer"

🎯The Task
Imagine you are a reporter interviewing a successful person. Speak with a friendly, high-energy voice.
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

Real-world communication is about asking questions and showing interest.

This is a Social Protocol. It practices the "Interrogative" (question) form of the Present Continuous tense while building the confidence to initiate a conversation.

📢 Speak these lines:
1. "Hello! Are you enjoying your day?"
2. "What are you working on at the moment?"
3. "I am looking for some advice on how to be brave."
4. "Thank you for sharing your thoughts with me!"

🔥 POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "Proficient "

Pronunciation: pruh-FISH-uhnt

Meaning: Very skilled.

Usage: "An engineer must be proficient in using AutoCAD software."

🔥 IDIOM OF THE DAY: "BITE THE BULLET"

Marathi Meaning: कठीण प्रसंगाचा धैर्याने सामना करणे.

⚙️ The Logic: Sometimes you have to do something difficult or unpleasant (like giving a speech in English). You just have to "bite the bullet" and do it.

✔ Example: "I am nervous about the presentation, but I will bite the bullet and start speaking."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Question Flip" (Present Continuous)


To turn a statement into a question, FLIP the subject and the helping verb.


• Statement: You are listening. → Question: Are you listening?


• Statement: It is working. → Question: Is it working?


⚠️ Activity: Flip these sentences into questions:


1. They are practising.

2. He is making a mistake.
