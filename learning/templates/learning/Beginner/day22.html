🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "UNPRECEDENTED"

Meaning: Never done or known before.

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Indianism" Preposition Fix (Part 2)

🔹 The Hack: Let's fix 3 more common errors often made by Indian speakers.

1. The "Wait For" vs. "Await" Mix-up:

"I am awaiting for the results."

"I am awaiting the results" OR "I am waiting for the results."

2. The "Cope Up" Error:

"It is hard to cope up with the pressure."

"It is hard to cope with the pressure."

3. The "Revert Back" Error:

"Please revert back to my email."

"Please revert to my email."
