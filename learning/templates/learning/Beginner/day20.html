🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Prepositions of Direction

These words describe Movement from one place to another.

🔹 The Hack:

• TO vs. TOWARDS: To = specific destination, Towards = general direction

• INTO vs. IN: In = static/inside, Into = movement to the inside

• THROUGH: Moving from one side to the other in an enclosed space

• ACROSS: Moving from one side to the other on a surface
