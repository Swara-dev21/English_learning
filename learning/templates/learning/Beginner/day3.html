🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Confidence Broadcast"

🎙️ Forget the mirror. Today, you are a Live News Reporter or a Technical Presenter.
🎯The Task
Stand up straight, take a deep breath, and "Broadcast" these lines like a reporter.
📢 Broadcast these lines:
1. “Good day! Today, I have an important update.”
2. “I observe my progress every single day.”
3. “My English skills improve as I speak to you now.”
4. “I perfect my habits, and I feel strong.”

✍️ 4. Writing Activity · Confidence Switch & Technical Identity

✨ Part A: The "Confidence Switch" (Guided)


1. I speak slowly, but I speak ____________________.

2. I am not perfect, but I am ____________________.

📝 Main Task: Write 3 things you do to stay confident daily using "I + Verb"


Example: I smile. I stand straight. I listen well.

⚙️ Part B: Technical Identity (Review)


1. I study __________ engineering.

2. I operate __________ (name a tool).

3. I analyze __________ (document/drawing).

🔥 IDIOM OF THE DAY: "PRACTICE WHAT YOU PREACH"

Marathi Meaning: उक्तीप्रमाणे कृती करणे (जे सांगतो तेच करून दाखवणे).


⚙️ The Logic: If a technical manual says a machine must run at 500 RPM, it must perform that way. If you tell yourself you are a learner, you must do the daily work.


✔ Example: “I tell my friends to be confident, so I must practice what I preach and speak up today!”


🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Simple Present (Universal Truths)


We use Simple Present for Facts and Universal Truths (things that never change).

• The Sun rises in the east.


• Water boils at 100°C.


• A computer processes data.


⚠️ Remember: Even for facts, the "Third-Person" rule applies (Sun/Water/Computer = It, so we add 's').
