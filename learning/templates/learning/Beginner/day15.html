🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Interview Entry"

The first 10 seconds of an interview are determined by your posture and your greeting
🎯The Task
Imagine you are walking into a cabin for a placement interview.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines with a firm but polite tone:
1
"Good morning. I am grateful for this opportunity."
2
"I will have perfected my technical demonstration by the time we begin." (Future Perfect!)
3
"I strive to maintain a poised appearance during high-pressure situations."(Smart substitute for calm)
4
"My demeanour reflects my professional values."(Smart substitute for way of behaving)
5
"By the end of this year, I will have attained my certification."
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

Focusing on your "demeanour" and "poise" forces you to think about your physical presence while speaking.

✍️ 4. Writing Activity · Future Perfect Goals

✨ Part A: Smart Substitutions

❌ Instead of "I will be ready,"

✅ Write: "I will have finalized my preparations."

✅ Write: "I maintain a poised demeanour."

✨ Rule: After "will have", always use the past participle (V3) of the verb.

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Future Perfect (The "Deadline" Tense)

Use this to talk about an action that will be finished at a specific point in the future. It always looks back from a future "deadline."

Formula: Subject + Will Have + V3 (Past Participle)

Key Marker: Usually uses the word "By" (By Monday, By 5 PM, By next year).

Action Deadline Future Perfect Sentence
Finish homework 8 PM I will have finished by 8 PM.
Graduate 2027 I will have graduated by 2027.

⚠️ Activity: Combine the Action and the Deadline using Future Perfect (Write the complete sentence):

1. I (complete) my training / By Friday.
2. They (repair) the machine / By tomorrow morning.
3. We (reach) Nashik / By 10 PM.
