The Exercise

2. Current Focus
"Currently, I am working towards mastering technical communication."
3. Recent Achievement
"I have finalized my recent project on time."
4. Values Statement
"I strive to exemplify the values of this company."
5. Future Goal
"By next quarter, I will have attained my lead certification."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Indianism" Preposition Fix

🔹 The Hack: Indian speakers often translate directly from mother tongues, leading to these common errors:

1. The "Discuss About" Error:

❌"Let's discuss about the project."

✅ ""Let's discuss the project." ('Discuss' means 'talk about', so 'about' is extra).

2. The "Order For" Error:

❌"I will order for a coffee."

✅ ""I will order a coffee."

3. The "Pick Up" Error:

❌"I will pick up you at 5 PM."

✅ ""I will pick you up at 5 PM."(The object goes in the middle)
