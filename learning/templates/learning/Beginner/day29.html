🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "SOPHISTICATED"

Pronunciation: Suh-FIS-ti-kay-ted

Meaning: Having a great deal of worldly experience and knowledge; complex.

Usage: "Your use of vocabulary has become much more sophisticated over the last 29 days."

🧠 Grammar Hack: The "Power of Passive" for Diplomacy

🔹 The Hack: Sometimes, using "I" or "You" sounds like you are blaming someone. Use the Passive Voice to stay professional.

❌ Aggressive: "You made a mistake in the report."

✅ Diplomatic: "A mistake was made in the report."

❌ Aggressive: "I want you to finish this."

✅ Professional: "It is requested that this be finished."

✍️ Activity: Make these sentences more diplomatic

2. "I think your idea is bad."
