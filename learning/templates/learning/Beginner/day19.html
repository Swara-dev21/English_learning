🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "COMPASSIONATE"

Pronunciation: /kəmˈpæʃənət/ (Kum-PASH-un-it)

Meaning: Feeling or showing sympathy and concern for others.

Usage: "A compassionate listener understands the speaker's stress as well as their words."

💡Idiom of the Day: "Bridge the gap"

Marathi Meaning: दोन गोष्टींमधील अंतर किंवा फरक कमी करणे.

⚙️ The Logic: Active listening helps you "bridge the gap" between what a client wants and what you, as an engineer, actually build.

Example: "The manager's job is to bridge the gap between the technical team and the customers."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Prepositions of Place (In, On, At)

Think of space like a target going from Specific to General.

🔹 The Hack:

• AT (The Point): Specific location or point

  • ✓ At the door
  • ✓ At the bus stop
  • ✓ At the corner
  • ✓ At the top of the page

• ON (The Surface): Attached to or touching a surface

  • ✓ On the wall
  • ✓ On the table
  • ✓ On the floor
  • ✓ On the menu

• IN (The Container): Inside an enclosed space or large area

  • ✓ In the box
  • ✓ In the room
  • ✓ In Nashik
  • ✓ In the car

⚠️ Exceptional Examples:

1. At the desk (Sitting there to work)
2. In the corner of a room VS On the corner of a street
3. At school/work (A general place/activity) VS In the school building (The physical structure)
