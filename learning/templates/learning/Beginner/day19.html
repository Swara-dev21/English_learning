🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Prepositions of Place (In, On, At)

Think of space like a target going from Specific to General.

🔹 The Hack:

• AT (The Point): Specific location or point (At the door, At the bus stop, At the corner, At the top of the page)

• ON (The Surface): Attached to or touching a surface (On the wall, On the table, On the floor, On the menu)

• IN (The Container): Inside an enclosed space or large area (In the box, In the room, In Nashik, In the car)

⚠️ Exceptional Examples:

• At the desk (Sitting there to work)

• In the corner of a room VS On the corner of a street

• At school/work (A general place/activity) VS In the school building (The physical structure)
