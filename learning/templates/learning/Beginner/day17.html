🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "UNWAVERING"

Pronunciation: /ʌnˈweɪvərɪŋ/ (Un-WAY-ver-ing)

Meaning: Steady and firm; not changing.

Usage: "He maintained unwavering eye contact during the entire interview."

💡Idiom of the Day: "See eye to eye"

Marathi Meaning: पूर्णपणे सहमत असणे.

⚙️ The Logic: When two people look at each other and agree on a plan, they "see eye to eye."

Example: "My boss and I don't always see eye to eye on the design, but we respect each other."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The Advanced Article Compass

Knowing when NOT to use an article is as important as knowing when to use one.

🔹 The Hack:

• The "Zero Article" Rule (No A/An/The):

   ✓ General Plurals: I like — cars. (Not "The cars" in general)

   ✓ Abstract Ideas: I value — honesty. (Not "The honesty")

   ✓ Proper Names: I live in — Nashik. (Not "The Nashik")

• The "Unique" The: Use for things that are "only one": The Sun, The Principal, The Internet

• The "Mentioned Before" Rule: I have a plan. The plan is simple.
