🔥 IDIOM OF THE DAY: "PRACTICE MAKES PERFECT"

Marathi Meaning: सराव केल्याने परिपूर्णता येते.


⚙️ The Logic: Just like a machine needs "running-in" time to work smoothly, your brain needs repetition to become fluent.


✔ Example: "I practice my presentation daily because practice makes perfect."


🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Present Simple (The "Always" Tense)

Use for daily habits or technical facts (SOPs).

🔹 The Rule:

• I / You / We / They → Study

• He / She / It → Studies (Add 's' or 'es')

🔹 Negative Form:

• I do not (don't) study.

• The motor does not (doesn't) start.

⚠️ Activity: Write complete sentences:


1. She (study) __________ engineering every day.


2. The machine (not work) __________ without calibration.


3. They (follow) __________ a strict routine.
