🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "INDEFATIGABLE"

Pronunciation: In-de-FAT-i-ga-bul

Meaning: Persisting tirelessly; never giving up.

Usage: "His indefatigable efforts in the lab led to a breakthrough."

💬Idiom of the Day: "Barking up the wrong tree"

Marathi Meaning: चुकीच्या ठिकाणी शोध घेणे किंवा चुकीचा अंदाज लावणे.

⚙️ The Logic: If you are trying to fix a software bug by changing the hardware, you are "barking up the wrong tree."

Example: "If you think the problem is the battery, you're barking up the wrong tree; it's a wiring issue."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Indianism" Preposition Fix (Part 4)

🔹 The Hack: Let's finish our prepositional "cleanup" with these 3 tricky ones.

1. The "Angry on" Error:

"The boss is angry on me."

"The boss is angry with me." (Use 'with' for people, 'at' for things)

2. The "Prepone" Error:

"Can we prepone the meeting?"

"Can we move the meeting forward?"

3. The "Passed out" Error:

"I passed out from college in 2022."

"I graduated from college in 2022."
