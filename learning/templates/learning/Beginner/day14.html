🔥POWER WORD OF THE WEEK: "RESILIENT"

Pronunciation: /rɪˈzɪliənt/ (Re-ZIL-yent)

Meaning: Able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions.

Usage: "The Week 2 students are resilient; they never let a difficult word stop them."

💡Idiom of the Day: "Under the weather"

Meaning: तब्येत ठीक नसणे.

The Logic: You don't need to learn 1000 words in one day. Learning 5 words every day is what actually wins the "fluency race."

Example: "I feel under the weather today."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Future Continuous (The "Ongoing Future")

Use this to talk about an action that will be in progress at a certain time in the future. It sounds more "active" and "polite" than Simple Future.

Formula: Subject + Will Be + Verb-ing

Example: "At 10 AM tomorrow, I will be attending my lecture."

Simple Future (Result) Future Continuous (Process)
I will finish the work. I will be finishing the work tomorrow.
We will meet. We will be meeting in the cafeteria.
