🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity : The "Technical Briefing"

When an engineer or a senior gives you instructions, you must ensure you have the correct data.
🎯The Task
Imagine a teacher or a clerk just gave you a very fast explanation about a form or a lab experiment.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines out loud. Focus on your intonation—keep it professional:
1
"I apologize, but I didn't quite grasp the last point."
2
"Could you please elaborate on the safety procedures?"
3
"To ensure I have this correct, you want me to...?"
4
"I appreciate your patience while I verify these details."
5
"Could you provide a brief summary of the main requirements?"
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

These phrases show that you are diligent. It is a sign of high intelligence to ask for clarification, not a sign of weakness.

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Simple Past vs. Present Perfect

🔹 The Hack: This is the most important choice in professional English!

Tense When to use? Key Words Example
Simple Past Finished time. ("Dead" Past) Yesterday, Last week. I finished the report yesterday.
Present Perfect Unfinished time / Experience. So far, Today, Yet. I have finished 3 reports so far.
The Choice Rule: If you say WHEN it happened (yesterday), use Simple Past. If time is NOT mentioned or is still happening (today), use Present Perfect.
