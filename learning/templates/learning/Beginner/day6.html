🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Professional Intro"


In daily life, the most important communication is introducing yourself.

🎯The Task
Imagine you are at a seminar or meeting a new group of people.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines out loud. Replace the bracketed info with your details, but keep the Strong Tone:
1
"Good morning/afternoon! My name is [Your Name]."
2
"I have pursued a diploma in [Branch] engineering." (Instead of "I did")
3
"Currently, I am exploring new ways to enhance my communication."
4
"I have dedicated myself to daily practice."
5
"It is a pleasure to interact with you all!"
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

This moves beyond "basic" English. Using words like pursued, enhance, and dedicated makes you sound like a high-level professional immediately.

🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "ELOQUENT"

Pronunciation: EL-oh-kwent

Meaning: Fluent or persuasive in speaking or writing.

Usage: "By practicing daily, you will become an eloquent speaker."

💬Idiom of the Day: "The best of both worlds"

Marathi Meaning: दोन्ही बाजूने फायदा होणे.

The Logic: If you have technical skills AND good English, you have "the best of both worlds."

✔ Example: "By studying engineering in English, I get the best of both worlds."

🧠 Grammar Hack: Present Perfect (The "Experience" Tense)

🔹 The Hack: Use this to talk about things you have finished, but the time is not important.

Formula: Subject + has/have + V3 (Past Participle)

I / You / We / They → have + V3

He / She / It → has + V3
