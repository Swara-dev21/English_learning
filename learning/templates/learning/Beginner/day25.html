🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Softening Phrases for Persuasion

🔹 The Hack: If you want to persuade a senior, don't use "I want" or "You are wrong." Use Softening Phrases.

1. Instead of "I disagree":

"I perceive it slightly differently..."

"That's a valid point, however..."

2. Instead of "Do this":

"I would suggest that we..."

"It might be beneficial if we..."

3. Instead of "I don't know":

"I will scrutinize that and revert to you."
