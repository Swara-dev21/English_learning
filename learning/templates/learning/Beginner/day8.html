🎧 1. Listening Activity : Polite Inquiry & Rising Intonation

💡 Before you speak, you must hear. This activity focuses on Polite Inquiry and Rising Intonation.
🎯The Task
Listen to the "Master Audio" and focus on the pauses (...).
📝Listening Text
“Excuse me… can you help me… please… I want to learn… and I am not shy to ask… Because asking questions… makes me better… 👉 Questions lead to knowledge.”

Play the audio and speak at the same pace. Notice how the voice goes up at the end to sound polite.

🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Help Desk"

🎙️ Imagine you are lost in a new city or confused in a new office.
🎯The Task
Imagine you are lost in a new city or confused in a new office.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines out loud with a clear, friendly, and bold voice:
1
"Excuse me, I require a moment of your time."
2
"Could you please clarify how this system works?"
3
"I am seeking the entrance to the main hall."
4
"I appreciated your help earlier today."
5
"Would you mind assisting me with this task?"
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

Using "Could you" and "Would you" instead of "Can you" immediately upgrades your social status and makes people more likely to help you.

📖 Simple Past Verbs

Definition:
Simple past verbs are used to describe actions that already happened in the past.

Example:

Pronunciation: Uh-PREE-she-uh-tiv

Regular Verbs: Just add -ed
