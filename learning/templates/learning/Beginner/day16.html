🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The Article Compass (A, An, The)

Articles define whether a noun is Specific or General.

🔹 The Hack:

• A / An (General): Use for "one" out of many.

    A: Before consonant sounds (A car, A university — 'U' sounds like 'Y')

    An: Before vowel sounds (An apple, An hour — 'H' is silent)

• The (Specific): Use when the listener knows exactly which one you mean.

   ✓ I saw a car. The car was red.

⚠️ Exceptional Cases (The "Sound" Rule):

It's about the SOUND, not the letter!

• An Honest man (Silent H)

• An M.B.A. student ('M' sounds like 'Em' - vowel sound)

• A European city ('E' sounds like 'Y' - consonant sound)
