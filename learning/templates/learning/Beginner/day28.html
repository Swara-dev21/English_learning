🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Comma Splice" Fix

The "Comma Splice" Fix

The Hack: Use a Semicolon (;) or a Period (.) instead of a comma to join two full sentences.

"I checked the report, it looks good."

"I checked the report. It looks good."

"I checked the report; it looks impeccable."
