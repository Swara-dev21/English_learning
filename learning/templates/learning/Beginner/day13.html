🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Professor's Guidance"

Talking to a teacher about your future plans requires a blend of respect and ambition.
🎯The Task
Imagine you are visiting your Professor's office to discuss your career path after graduation.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines out loud. Focus on the "Will + Verb" for future certainty:
1
You: "Good afternoon, Sir. I will aspire to join a core engineering firm." (Smart substitute for hope)
2
Professor: "That requires a lot of technical expertise."
3
You: "I understand. I will dedicate two hours every day to my practical skills."
4
Professor: "How will you manage your communication skills?"
5
You: "I will persist with my daily English routine. It will yield results soon."
6
Professor: "Good. That will ensure your success in the interviews."
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

This moves beyond "basic" English. Using words like pursued, enhance, and dedicated makes you sound like a high-level professional immediately.

🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "UNAVOIDABLE"

Pronunciation: /ʌnəˈvɔɪdəbəl/ (Un-a-VOID-a-bul)

Meaning: Impossible to avoid or prevent.

Usage: "With your current hard work, success is unavoidable."

💡Idiom of the Day: "Slow and steady wins the race"

Meaning: संयम आणि सातत्य यशाकडे नेते.

The Logic: You don't need to learn 1000 words in one day. Learning 5 words every day is what actually wins the "fluency race."

Example: "Don't rush your studies; remember, slow and steady wins the race."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Simple Future (The "Vision" Tense)

Use this for promises, plans, and predictions about the future.

Formula: Subject + Will + Base Verb (Action)

Note: Never add "-ed" or "-ing" after will. (Wrong: I will played. Right: I will play.)

UsageExample
PromiseI will practice every day.
PredictionIt will rain tomorrow.
DecisionI will call you later.
