🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Counter Clerk" Challenge

In daily life, you often need to communicate with people behind a desk—at a library, a bank, or a college counter.
🎯The Task
Imagine you are at the College Admin Counter. You need to collect a document, but there is a problem.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines out loud. Focus on being assertive (firm but polite):
1
"Good morning. I am here to collect my original leaving certificate."
2
"I submitted my application form last Friday."
3
"Could you please verify if my documents are ready?"
4
"I pardon the interruption, but I have a class commencing in five minutes."
5
"I am grateful for your assistance in this matter."
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

This is a Communicative Task. It forces you to combine the "Polite Inquiry" from Day 8 with real-world pressure.

🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "PROACTIVE"

Pronunciation: Pro-AK-tiv

Meaning: Creating or controlling a situation rather than just responding to it.

Usage: "A proactive student asks questions before the exam starts."

💬Idiom of the Day: "Break the ice"

Marathi Meaning: संवादाला सुरुवात करणे (शांतता भंग करून बोलू लागणे).

⚙️ The Logic: When a classroom is silent, the first person to ask a question is the one who "breaks the ice."

Example: "I asked a simple question just to break the ice in the meeting."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Past State" (Was vs. Were)

🔹 The Hack: Use these to describe how things were in the past (Status/Condition).

Subject → Past Verb

I / He / She / It → Was

You / We / They → Were

"I was shy." / "They were helpful."

✍️ Activity: Fill in the blanks with "Was" or "Were"
