🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "SYNERGY"

Meaning: The interaction of two or more agents to produce a combined effect greater than the sum of their separate effects.

Usage: "There is a great synergy between our technical skills and our communication practice."

💬Idiom of the Day: "Kill two birds with one stone"

Marathi Meaning: एका दगडात दोन पक्षी मारणे

Example: "Reading technical manuals in English helps me kill two birds with one stone."

🧠 Grammar Hack: The "Timeline" Hack (Present Perfect)

🔹 The Hack: If you want to say how long something has been happening until NOW, use "for" or "since"

Since: Use for a Starting Point (Since Monday, Since 9 AM).

"I have practiced since Monday."

For: Use for a Duration (For 7 days, For 2 hours).

"I have practiced for one week."
