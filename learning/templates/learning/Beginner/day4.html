👂 THE POWER OF LISTENING

💡 "To speak well, you must first learn to hear what is not being said."

🎧 1. Listening Activity · Focus and Retention

💡 Before you speak, you must hear. This activity focuses on Focus and Retention.
🎯The Task
Listen to the "Master Audio" and focus on the steady calm pace, then shadow and record.
📝Listening Text
“I listen carefully… to every word… Because good listening… builds good speaking… I focus… and understand better… 👉 Listening is the first step to communication.
🎙️ Record Your Voice
Listen back. Did you emphasize the word "focus" correctly?

🧠 Quick Listening Check


1. What is the "first step" to communication?

2. Why should we listen carefully?

🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Imagine You Are Me"

🎙️ Communication is about empathy—seeing the world through someone else's eyes.
🎯The Task
Think of someone you saw today (a shopkeeper, a bus driver, or a friend). Imagine what their day is like right now.
🗣️ The Exercise Speak
for 1 minute as if you are that person. Use the -ing rule (Present Continuous):
📢 Speak these lines as if you are that person:
1. "I am sitting in my shop right now."
2. "I am waiting for a customer to arrive."
3. "The sun is shining brightly outside."
4. "I am thinking about what to eat for lunch."
👉 Why this works: This is a Communicative Task. It forces you to move away from technical jargon and use "survival English"—the kind you use to connect with people in real life.

🔥 POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "Synthesize"

Pronunciation:SIN-thuh-size

Meaning: Combine different ideas to create something new. OR Combine ideas into one

Usage: "A pragmatic approach helps solve classroom issues."

🔥 IDIOM OF THE DAY: "READ BETWEEN THE LINES"

Marathi Meaning: शब्दांमागचा खरा अर्थ समजून घेणे.


⚙️ The Engineer's Logic: Sometimes a machine makes a small sound that isn't in the manual. An expert engineer "reads between the lines" to find the hidden problem.


✔ Example: "My boss didn't say he was unhappy, but I can read between the lines by looking at his email."


🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Present Continuous (The "Right Now" Tense)


Use this for actions happening at the moment of speaking.

Formula: Subject + am/is/are + (Verb + ing)


• I am speaking.


• He / She / It is working.


• You / We / They are listening.


⚠️ Remember: You must have the helping verb (am/is/are). You cannot say "I speaking." You must say "I am speaking."
