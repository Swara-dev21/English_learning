🔥 IDIOM OF THE DAY: "READ BETWEEN THE LINES"

Marathi Meaning: शब्दांमागचा खरा अर्थ समजून घेणे.


⚙️ The Engineer's Logic: Sometimes a machine makes a small sound that isn't in the manual. An expert engineer "reads between the lines" to find the hidden problem.


✔ Example: "My boss didn't say he was unhappy, but I can read between the lines by looking at his email."


🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Present Continuous (The "Right Now" Tense)


Use this for actions happening at the moment of speaking.


Formula: Subject + am/is/are + (Verb + ing)


• I am speaking.


• He / She / It is working.


• You / We / They are listening.


⚠️ Remember: You must have the helping verb (am/is/are). You cannot say "I speaking." You must say "I am speaking."
