💬Idiom of the Day: "Burn the midnight oil"

Marathi Meaning: खूप मेहनत करणे (रात्रभर जागून अभ्यास किंवा काम करणे).

The Logic: To achieve unprecedented results, sometimes you have to "burn the midnight oil" to finish a project.

Example: "I had to burn the midnight oil to get the blueprint ready for the morning meeting."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Indianism" Preposition Fix (Part 3)

🔹 The Hack: Let's tackle 3 more common errors unique to our region.

1. The "Myself" Error:

"Myself Rahul from Nashik."

"I am Rahul" OR "My name is Rahul." ('Myself' is a reflexive pronoun, it cannot be a subject).

2. The "Coming" vs. "Next" Error:

"I will finish it in the coming week."

"I will finish it next week."

3. The "Discussing on" Error:

"We are discussing on the issue."

"We are discussing the issue." (Never use 'on' after discuss).
