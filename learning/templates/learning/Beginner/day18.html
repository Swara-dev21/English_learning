🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The Time Anchors (At, On, In, Since, For)

Think of time like a target — it goes from Specific → General.

🔹 The Hack:

• AT (The Point): For specific times and moments — At 5:30 PM, At noon, At night

• ON (The Surface): For days and dates — On Friday, On 15th August, On my birthday

• IN (The Container): For months, years, seasons, parts of the day — In October, In 2026, In the morning

• SINCE: A specific starting point — Since 10 AM, Since Monday

• FOR: A duration/length of time — For 2 hours, For 5 days

⚠️ Exceptional Cases:

• We say "In the morning" but "At night."

• We say "On time" (exactly at the planned time) but "In time" (early enough).
