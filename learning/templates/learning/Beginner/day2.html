🔥 IDIOM OF THE DAY: "GET THE HANG OF IT"

Meaning: एखादी गोष्ट हळूहळू जमू लागणे.

When you first use a new machine, it feels difficult. After some time, you "get the hang of it".


Example: "English felt hard on Day 1, but I am slowly getting the hang of it."


🧠 6. Grammar Hack: The "Third-Person" Motor


🔹 The Rule: When talking about He, She, It, or a Single Machine, the verb needs an 's'.


1. The motor runs (Run + s).

2. My friend studies (Study + es).

3. The sensor works (Work + s).

⚠️ Warning: Do NOT add 's' for "I" or "You." (I work, not I works).
