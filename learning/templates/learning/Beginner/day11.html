🗣️ 2. Speaking Activity · The "Project Update"

🎯The Task
In an office or lab, people often ask, "What are you doing?" You must describe your ongoing actions clearly.
🗣️
The Exercise
Speak these lines out loud. Focus on the "-ing" flow:
1
"Thank you for the explanation! It was very insightful. Currently, I am implementing the changes we discussed."
2
"I am striving to complete the draft by this evening."
3
"I was analyzing the data when you arrived."
4
"I am truly indebted for your guidance."
5
"Thank you for your assistance. I will keep you updated on my progress."
💡 WHY THIS WORKS

Combining gratitude with a status update shows that you are proactive and that you value the other person's time.

🔥POWER WORD OF THE DAY: "RECIPROCAL"

Pronunciation: /rɪˈsɪprəkəl/ (Re-SIP-ro-kal)

Meaning: Given, felt, or done in return.

Usage: "Good communication is a reciprocal process between two people."

💡Idiom of the Day: "Give credit where credit is due"

Meaning: ज्याचे काम आहे त्याला त्याचे श्रेय देणे.

Example: "He did most of the work, so I must give credit where credit is due."

🧠 6. Grammar Hack: Past Continuous (The "Background" Tense)

Use this to describe an action that was in the middle of happening at a specific point in the past.

SubjectHelping VerbVerb + -ingExample
I / He / She / ItWasWorkingI was working at 5 PM.
You / We / TheyWereWorkingThey were working hard.
